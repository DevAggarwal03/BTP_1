# Quantum Prototype Calculation Block

## What does this block do?

In traditional "Few-Shot Prototypical Networks," a **prototype** is simply the average (mean) vector of all the sample embeddings for a specific class. If you have 5 sample vectors for the "Sports" class, you average those 5 vectors to get a single vector representing "Sports".

In our **Quantum Prototypical Network**, the embeddings are not classical vectors; they are **quantum states** generated by the Variational Quantum Circuit (VQC).

To find the average of these quantum states, this block performs the following:
1. **Takes the pure quantum states** (`|ψ⟩`) of the support set samples for a given class.
2. **Computes their outer products** to form density matrices (`|ψ⟩⟨ψ|`).
3. **Averages them together** to form a single **mixed state** density matrix (`ρ`). 

This mixed state `ρ` is our new **Quantum Class Prototype**.

## Why is it important?
By representing a class prototype as a mixed state instead of a pure state or a classical vector, the model can capture the intrinsic quantum uncertainty and variance of the data distribution, giving us a theoretically grounded quantum advantage in low-data regimes.
